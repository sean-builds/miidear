# The Grand Conservatory artwork

The Grand Conservatory is an original illustrative wedding setting, not a photograph of the configured venue. It combines an imagined candlelit glasshouse garden with a separately generated transparent botanical frame. Both were created for this project; no upstream repository's personal wedding images were reused.

- `assets/grand-conservatory-scene.webp`: original scene, converted from the generated PNG to WebP; 538,864 bytes.
- `assets/grand-floral-frame.webp`: original transparent botanical arch, retaining the generated alpha; 670,894 bytes. The central empty areas are genuinely transparent.
- `grand.js`: independently authored SVG gate, cartouche and engraved border; editable Fabric objects and local licensed font outlines.
- `grand.css`: a single user-triggered gate reveal, with instant keyboard/reduced-motion paths; no autoplay audio or infinite particle effects.

The template is available as **The Grand Conservatory**. Existing local projects remain as saved. A new template creates a separate document. Its flowers, imagery, lettering, crest, borders and text are individual editable objects. The original six suites remain available.

Core monogram letters stay vector paths. The floral frame is a transparent raster asset, and the scene is a raster illustration. PNG exports flatten the chosen page; SVG and project exports retain the representation selected by the author. Current-page SVG exports embed raster images and preserve vector ornament and outlined initials.

The illustrated revision adds atelier-botanical-arch.svg and atelier-garden-engraving.svg, authored as SVG paths using the original Miidear botanical and conservatory primitives. No raster generator or third-party artwork was used for these two assets.

## Portfolio-led Secret Garden

miidear-watercolor-garden.webp and miidear-watercolor-foreground.webp were generated with the built-in image tool using inspected Miidear portfolio contact sheets as material/style references. No client names, portraits or commissioned monograms were copied. The foreground has real alpha (center 0), and both assets are delivered as WebP. Prompts and direction are documented in PORTFOLIO-MOTION.md.
