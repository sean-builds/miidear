## Direction implemented

The Secret Garden uses evergreen ink, antique gold, cotton-paper ivory and layered watercolor gardens. The opening combines a gold vector crest, shaped double paper leaves, watercolor detail and an independent transparent floral foreground. The initial arched scene expands toward the viewport with native scroll progress. A second sticky scene has separately moving foreground/background and a rising curved paper surface. Chapter headings animate whole words, preserving joined calligraphy. Practical RSVP controls remain immediate. Reduced motion, Motion off and keyboard opening expose an unpinned, fully readable version.

The new template is one editable 700 × 1450 canvas. New artwork keys secret-scene and secret-frame preserve the asset mappings used by previously saved projects. Existing projects are not rewritten. The new painted assets appear in the editor library as well as the guest presentation. Wedding story, names, date, venue, practical information and RSVP remain bound to the wedding configuration.
